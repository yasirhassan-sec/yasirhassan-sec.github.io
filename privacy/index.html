Privacy

This site collects as little as possible. Here's exactly what happens to your data, in plain English.

The short version

  • No cookies, no advertising and no tracking scripts.
  • If you use the contact form, your message reaches me by email so I can reply. That's the only reason I collect it.
  • I never sell or share your details with anyone else.

What's collected and why

Contact form

When you send a message, the form collects your name, email address, subject and message. It's delivered through Formspree, which forwards it to my inbox. I use it only to read and reply to your message, and I don't add you to any mailing list.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. I don't have access to those logs.

Your preferences

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Analytics

Analytics are switched off. If I turn them on in future, I'll use GoatCounter, which counts page views without cookies or personal data, and I'll update this page first.

Things this site doesn't do

  • No cookies, fingerprinting or cross-site tracking.
  • No third-party fonts or embeds: fonts are served from this site.
  • No accounts or logins, so there's no profile stored about you.

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn and WhatsApp take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

To ask what I hold about you, or to have a message you sent deleted, email yasirb169@gmail.com. I'll deal with it promptly.
