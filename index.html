How I work

A structured approach to security assessment, guided by the OWASP Testing Guide and NIST practice.

  1. 01

    Scope & recon

    Agree what's in scope, then map the attack surface: hosts, services, endpoints and exposed data.

    • Nmap
    • OSINT
  2. 02

    Assess

    Test against the OWASP Top 10 with tooling and manual checks, so findings are real, not scanner noise.

    • Burp Suite
    • Wireshark
  3. 03

    Validate & prioritise

    Confirm impact, rate severity and rank issues by real business risk rather than by count.

    • CVSS
    • Threat modelling
  4. 04

    Report & remediate

    Write clear findings with evidence and fix steps developers can act on, then retest the fixes.

    • Reporting
    • Retesting

When I build software, I apply the same thinking from the start: least privilege, defence in depth and tamper evidence, as in Milkiyat and Dastarkhwan.
