Final Year Project · In progress

VigiLense: AI Surveillance & Incident Response

Bahria University · My role: detection lead · Team of 3

Monitors multiple camera feeds, detects persons, weapons, fire and smoke, scores incidents by priority, switches to the most important camera, and logs evidence-backed alerts on a live web dashboard.

  • Trained a YOLO26n fire & smoke model on the D-Fire dataset: 0.78 mAP50, 0.78 precision, 0.72 recall.
  • Built the end-to-end pipeline: video → detection → SQLite incident with snapshot → live web page (FastAPI).
  • Multi-camera support with batched detection, camera grid and priority-based automatic camera switching.
  • Next: weapon detection, ByteTrack tracking with cross-camera re-ID, and a React dashboard.

Milkiyat: Secure Blockchain Land Records

Desktop application · Python
  • Land registry on a persistent SHA-256 hash-chain ledger; each approved record is a block digitally signed with RSA-2048 (PSS).
  • AES-256-GCM encryption of owner and location data at rest; PBKDF2 password hashing and TOTP multi-factor authentication built from scratch.
  • Role-based access for Citizen, Registrar, Surveyor and Admin with separation of duties, a full audit log, and tamper detection that flags any edited block.
